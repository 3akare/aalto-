What a limited company actually is

A limited company is a legal person in its own right. It can hold money, sign contracts and be taken to court separately from the people who own it. That separation is the whole point, and it is called limited liability.

In practice it means that if the business cannot pay what it owes, the people who own it lose what they put in and no more. Their house is not on the table. A sole trader has no such line: the debts of the business are simply their debts.

Registration is not instant. Expect a decision within five working days of filing, and note that a name can be refused for being too similar to one already on the register, which is the most common reason an application comes back.

Once registered, the company must file accounts every year even if it traded nothing at all. Dormant accounts are short, but they are not optional, and the penalty for filing late starts at a hundred and fifty.

What the agent is doing

    Press the button, then say these out loud, in order. It is a one-minute tour, and the last two are the interesting part - it will read the form back before it will submit it.

      Seven things you can do

      Each of these is a tool the agent can reach for on its own. One sentence often uses several of them, and you can interrupt at any point.

      Dictate anywhere

      Speak into any box on any site. It writes what you meant, punctuated, in the register of wherever it is going - and edits what is already there when you ask.

      "Actually, make that shorter and less formal."

      Fill any form

      One sentence fills several fields. It reads every answer back including the blanks, asks when a value matches no option, and refuses to submit what you have not heard.

      "I'm a sole trader, retail, and I have photo ID."

      Ask the page

      Questions about what is in front of you get answered from the page itself, and it scrolls to the sentence it used.

      "How long does this take?"

      Drive your tabs

      Open, switch and close by description. Anything it opens goes behind what you are already reading.

      "Close the research tabs."

      Work on a selection

      Highlight anything and have it summarised to your clipboard, rewritten in plain language, or replaced in place.

      "Put that in three bullets."

      Chain it, and interrupt it

      One instruction can be several actions. If you change your mind halfway through a sentence, say so - it stops talking and takes the new instruction, mid-word.

      "No - the other one."

      Teach it once

      Save a sequence you do often under a name you choose, then ask for it by that name whenever you want it again.

      "Remember that as my morning setup."

      Install the extension

      Two minutes, no store listing to wait on. Chrome, Edge, or any Chromium browser.

      1. 1 Download and unzip

        aalto-1.0.0.zip - put it somewhere you will not delete by accident. The extension loads from that folder, so moving it later means loading it again.

      2. 2 Open your extensions page

        Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, top right. The next button only appears once that is on.

      3. 3 Load unpacked

        Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder - the one with manifest.json directly inside it, not its parent.

      4. 4 Grant the microphone once

        Press Alt+A. The first time, Aalto opens a page asking for the microphone - it has to ask from a real page, because the invisible document that does the listening may use the microphone but may not prompt for it.

      5. 5 Start talking

        Alt+A anywhere starts a conversation, and pressing it again ends one. Rebind it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
